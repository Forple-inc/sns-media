Forple Publisher

Forple Publisher is an internal tool of Forple Inc. It uploads the videos made by our team to our own YouTube channel, Speak Japanese With Class, at a scheduled time, and sends our own short videos to our own TikTok account as drafts. It is used only by Forple Inc. staff and is not offered to the public.

This tool uses the YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3: videos.insert, thumbnails.set).
