Forple Publisher Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Forple Publisher (the "Tool") is an internal tool of Forple Inc. ("we", "us"). Our own staff use it to upload videos that our team has made to our own YouTube channel, "Speak Japanese With Class", and to send our own short videos to our own TikTok account as drafts. The Tool is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data the Tool uses and how.

1. YouTube API Services

The Tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using the Tool, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data that Google receives through the YouTube API Services is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

2. What data the Tool accesses

  • Google / YouTube: a sign-in token for our own channel's Google Account, with the single permission "Manage your YouTube videos" (youtube.upload). With it, the Tool uploads a video file with its title, description, tags, translations of the title and description, and thumbnail (videos.insert, thumbnails.set). The Tool does not read any other data from YouTube.
  • TikTok: a sign-in token for our own TikTok account with the permissions user.info.basic and video.upload, used only to send our own videos to that account's inbox as drafts.

The Tool does not access, collect or store any data about other YouTube or TikTok users, viewers or channels.

3. How the data is used, stored and shared

  • The sign-in tokens are used only to upload our own videos to our own accounts.
  • They are stored only on one company computer, in files that only that computer's user can read. They are never put on a server, in source code or in a shared folder.
  • The Tool keeps a log of our own uploads (date, video ID) on the same computer.
  • We do not sell or share this data with anyone. We do not use cookies or analytics in the Tool.

4. How to revoke access and delete data

  • Access to the Google Account can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. TikTok access can be removed in the TikTok app's settings (Settings and privacy → Security → the list of apps and services with access).
  • When we stop using the Tool, or when access is revoked, we delete the stored tokens from the computer within 7 days. Tokens that are not refreshed expire on their own.
  • To ask for deletion or to ask a question, contact us at the address below.

5. Changes

If we change how the Tool uses data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
